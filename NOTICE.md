Zynerd, MCC, NMC, or NBEMS.

## Data (not covered by the MIT license grant)

The JSON datasets in `dist/` (`data.json`, `pg-records-*.json`,
`pg-zero-*.json`, `state-records-*.json`, `financial-records-*.json`) are
factual extracts compiled from publicly published official documents —
primarily MCC NEET PG seat matrices and final allotment results, plus
state-authority and institutional publications. Exact source URLs, retrieval
dates, extraction methods, and reconciliation notes are recorded in
[data-provenance.txt](data-provenance.txt) and
[sources/pg-content-provenance.txt](sources/pg-content-provenance.txt).

The MIT license above covers this project's code and its original
compilation/structure. It does not grant rights in the underlying official
publications themselves, which remain the property of their publishing
authorities. Always verify seats, cutoffs, fees, bonds, eligibility, and
dates against the current official publication before acting. Historical
cutoffs describe past allotments; they are not predictions or guarantees of
admission. A saved or exported choice list is a planning aid only — it does
not submit or lock choices on any official portal.

If you redistribute the datasets, keep the provenance files and source dates
with them, and do not present dated snapshots as live vacancy data.
