ZYJOCK DATA ACQUISITION AND COVERAGE
Acquired: 2026-10-19
Public website data file: dist/data.json
Reproduction: python sources/build_data.py (run from zyjock/)

VERIFIED HISTORICAL SCOPE
20 institutions listed in MCC's one-page FINAL SEAT MATRIX FOR AIIMS/BHU/JIMPER ROUND 1 UG 2025 (MBBS & BDS). Only MBBS, NEET UG, MCC, Open Seat Quota, 2025, Round 1 are bundled as verified. This is not an exhaustive national, all-quota, all-round or all-exam dataset.

PRIMARY SOURCES
1. MCC archive page, which links all three PDFs below:
   https://mcc.nic.in/archive-ug/
2. Seat matrix uploaded July 22, 2025:
   https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2025/07/2025072296.pdf
3. Final Result for Round-I of NEET UG Counselling 2025, August 13, 2025:
   https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2025/08/20250813289226788.pdf
4. MCC correction, August 13, 2025, removing one SC PwD seat at AIIMS New Delhi:
   https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2025/08/20250813868365001.pdf

METHOD
- Downloaded public official PDFs; retained original PDFs and pdftotext -layout text in sources/.
- Parsed 20 matrix rows by MCC institute code; verified each row sums to its published total.
- Applied the specific AIIMS New Delhi SC PwD correction: July value 1 becomes 0. Original values remain documented in the record notes and linked source.
- Selected only corresponding institutions, MBBS and Open Seat Quota in the final allotment result.
- Aggregated minimum and maximum rank by institute and ALLOTTED category. No individual candidate records, names, roll numbers or application identifiers are in dist/data.json.
- Preserved the distinction between allotted seat category and candidate category. Handled wrapped PwD cells using the original table column positions. Using candidate category instead would produce incorrect cutoffs.
- Normalized matrix General-EWS to EWS, matching the result document; preserved originalCategory in each record. Open means the open seat category, not solely General-category candidates.
- City/name spelling is normalized for display; source institution codes are retained. Unverified university and institution-website fields remain null.
- openingRank / closingRank are DERIVED historical aggregates of allotted candidates. They are not MCC-published forecast probabilities, current vacancies, admissions guarantees or joined-candidate cutoffs.

RECONCILIATION
200 institution/category pools across 20 institutions.
2,178 historical allotments in the included institutions.
2,178 seats after applying the documented deletion.
Every individual institution/category pool's allotment count equals its corrected matrix count.
174 pools have at least one allotted rank; 26 zero-seat pools correctly have null ranks.
No unreconciled pool remains in this bounded dataset.
PDF SHA-256 hashes and acquisition counts are saved in sources/acquisition-summary.json.

UNVERIFIED FIELDS
No verified annual fee, stipend, service bond, bond penalty, previous-year seats, score-to-rank curve, current vacancy or new-seat change is bundled. Those fields are null in all official records. Academic rules and requirements must be checked against the current session's official sources.

DEMONSTRATION DATA
Eight explicitly fictional institutions have names beginning 'Demo ·'. The dataStatus is demo; verified is false; sourceUrl is null. The 1,440 generated records span six exams, 2024/2025, three rounds and five categories to demonstrate filtering, comparisons, choice lists, fee/bond tools and trends. These are not admissions facts and must remain opt-in / visibly identified in the UI. The generated rankCurves are also demo-only.

RESOURCES AND NOTICES
Official portal links were browsed on acquisition day. Notices are links to official notice boards, with date=null; they are not fabricated deadlines and the app does not claim a live synchronized notice feed. A dated historical correction is listed separately. No private Zynerd API or database was accessed or used.
