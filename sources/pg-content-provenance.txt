NBEMS RESULT — 2026 SCORE SCALE
Original NBEMS notice dated 2026-09-24, mirrored by the official Gujarat
admission authority and linked from https://www.medadmgujarat.org/pg/home.aspx:
https://medadmgujarat.ncode.in/web/PG2026/MDMS/NBEMS_2026619243078.PDF
English pages 3–4 explicitly state scores OUT OF 720. Initial qualifying
scores: General/EWS 262; General PwBD 244; SC/ST/OBC including PwBD 226.
These are qualification thresholds, not college admission cut-offs or a
score-to-rank curve. The 2025 maximum of 800 must not be carried into 2026.
Direct NBEMS access was intermittently unavailable; the host/publisher
distinction is explicit in the source metadata.
